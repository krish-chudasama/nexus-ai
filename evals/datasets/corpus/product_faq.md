# Product FAQ

## Pricing plans
The Starter plan costs 12 dollars per seat per month and includes up to 5 projects. The Pro plan costs 29 dollars per seat per month and includes unlimited projects and priority email support. The Enterprise plan uses custom pricing and adds single sign-on (SSO) and a dedicated account manager.

## API limits
The Pro plan allows 600 API requests per minute per workspace. Starter workspaces are limited to 60 requests per minute. Requests above the limit receive an HTTP 429 response with a Retry-After header.

## Data retention
Deleted projects are kept in a recoverable state for 30 days and are then permanently removed. Audit logs are retained for 12 months on the Enterprise plan only.

## Support hours
Email support is answered Monday to Friday between 09:00 and 18:00 UTC. Enterprise customers have access to 24/7 phone support for severity 1 incidents.
