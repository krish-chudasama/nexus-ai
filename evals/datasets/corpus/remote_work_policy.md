# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility and schedule
Full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation may work remotely up to three days per week. Managers approve the weekly schedule, and the team must keep at least two in-office days that overlap.

## Core hours
All remote employees must be reachable during core hours, which are 10:00 to 15:00 India Standard Time. Meetings outside core hours must be agreed in advance by every attendee.

## Equipment stipend
Each employee receives a one-time home-office equipment stipend of 40,000 rupees. The stipend can be used for a monitor, chair, desk, or keyboard. Receipts must be submitted to Finance within 30 days of purchase.

## Security requirements
Remote work requires the company VPN to be active whenever internal systems are accessed. Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Public Wi-Fi may only be used together with the VPN.
